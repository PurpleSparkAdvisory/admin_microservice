The Say–Do Gap

What you're looking at. Every leader is measured on the same set of leadership traits, two ways: what they say they value — drawn from their own written reflections — and what they do when they make decisions under pressure in timed simulations. Both are scored on the same 0–10 scale. Where the two pull apart is the leader's say–do gap: the traits they talk about but don't act on, or act on without naming.
